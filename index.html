Questions

Before you book

What kind of band is Machine Gun Rabbit?

A Calgary party band. We play the covers everyone knows — from ABBA to AC/DC, 50+ sing-along songs a night. Not a concert, a party.

Where are you based, and where do you play?

Calgary, Alberta. We play Calgary and area, including Langdon, Elbow River Casino, The Kings Head and Big Al's.

Can we book you for a wedding, party or private event?

Yes — weddings, private parties, bars and festivals. Send us a DM on Instagram to book. A booking email is on the way.

What songs do you play?

Sing-along anthems across every era: Mr. Brightside, Highway to Hell, Mamma Mia, Teenage Dirtbag, and 50+ more.
